# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the GazeAwake community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and technically honest.
- Welcome questions and different experience levels.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, especially when discussing camera-derived data.
- Correct misleading performance or accuracy claims openly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, publishing private information, sexualized conduct, sustained disruption, or deliberately misleading security/privacy claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through GitHub. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.
